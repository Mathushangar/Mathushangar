WHY I DO WHAT I DO

It started with a failure.

My first business failed early in my entrepreneurial journey. It set me back by several years, financially, but also personally.

I learned that business failure doesn't stay inside the business. It follows the entrepreneur home. It can affect confidence, relationships, decisions and the way you see your own future.

I couldn't rewrite that failure. But I could decide what came next.

I wanted to build my own success story, while becoming part of the growth stories of other entrepreneurs.

That decision gradually became the purpose behind almost everything I do today.
